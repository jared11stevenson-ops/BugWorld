# Wardchar

A cleansing line sweeps the world on a fixed cadence. Fire does not ask who is guilty; there is a narrow gap, and the wise learn where it opens.

## Inspirations (real world)
- **humoral medicine and purging** — Historic European medicine often tried to restore the body's balance by purging and bloodletting, practices now known to be ineffective or harmful.
- **forest fire ecology** — Some forests and their species depend on periodic fire to clear undergrowth and release seeds.
- **John Snow's cholera map** — In 1854 John Snow mapped London cholera deaths around the Broad Street pump, helping show that the disease spread through water.
