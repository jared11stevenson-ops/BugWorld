# Puddlespike

The air here is never still. Gusts rise without warning and carry everything that flies; the clever play the wind rather than fight it.

## Inspirations (real world)
- **water balloon fights** — A water balloon bursts on impact when its thin rubber skin is stretched past its limit.
- **volleyball** — Volleyball was invented in 1895 by William G. Morgan in Holyoke, Massachusetts.
