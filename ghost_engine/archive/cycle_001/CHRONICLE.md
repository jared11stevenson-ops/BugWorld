# Clocktimber

Everything in this universe beats with a rhythm; periodic waves sweep across the land and punish those who ignore the beat.

## Inspirations (real world)
- **predator-prey cycles** — Predator and prey populations often rise and fall in lagged cycles.
- **circadian rhythms** — Most organisms keep roughly 24-hour internal rhythms synchronized by light.
- **Easter Island's deforestation** — Rapa Nui lost most of its palm forest, a contested but widely discussed case of resource depletion.
