# UNDERSTUDY — The Thirteen Lives

> The thirteen people the player wakes up as, one per day, in canonical order. Each was Taken at 4:12 a.m. on the day the player wears them, after a Decision made the evening before.
> **Spoilers throughout.** Cross-reference `01-canon.md` for rules and numbers.

## How to read an entry

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Truth** | Human at the time of the Take. Afterwards, the player's Remnant becomes a walk-on. |
| **Resonance** | The drowned person whose memory leads the Chorus that day. Always kin or counterpart. |
| **Face / Secret / Stance** | Public self; what they hide; their view of change. |
| **Decision** | What they decided, and why the lake heard it. |
| **Wake & Snoop** | What's in the home. *Every home has a red ribbon on the bedroom doorknob (Nell's set dressing).* The pet reacts. |
| **Perform** | The job vignette and its mechanic. |
| **Bleed** | The habit that leaks into later roles. |
| **Arc** | Before → your day → after (the Remnant) → finale. |
| **Imprint / reacts** | How the Remnant develops according to the day's **Notes**, and how this person reacts to your other performances. |
| **If returned** | What the original says when they wake on the shore (Endings 2 and 4). |

### The five Notes
After each day the Chorus is scored on five Notes. These are the "director's notes" that harden into the Remnant's personality.

**WARMTH** (kindness vs cool) · **CANDOR** (honest vs guarded) · **NERVE** (bold vs timid) · **CURIOSITY** (asks vs coasts) · **FIDELITY** (stays in character vs improvises)

## 1. Dr. Margaret "Mags" Pell — dentist, 44 · Day 1, Tue 27 Aug

**Truth:** Human; Index Club. Granddaughter of Casimir. **Resonance:** *Dr. Casimir Pell, 63*, Low Mercy dentist. On the night of the flood he stayed late to pull a tooth for a nine-year-old with an ache, because the boy's parents were still on the way. The boy was Amos Marsh. The chart is dated 9 Sept 1961 and reads *"paid in eggs."*

**Face:** Brisk, punny, hums while she drills, keeps a bowl of mints "for the brave." Calls every patient "friend".
**Secret:** For two years she's been matching her patients' X-rays against her grandfather's 1950s charts. She found **eleven matches**: fillings and crowns Casimir placed on people who were *not alive to receive them*. She has told only the Index Club (Priya, Vern, Cal), and Delphine, the day before, in a note. Mags now knows that some neighbours are the drowned.
**Stance on change:** Terrified of staying, ashamed of being terrified.
**Decision:** At 9:47 p.m. on Mon 26 Aug she texted a buyer: *"Yes. Close Friday."* Bus ticket on the 6:40 launch.

**Wake & Snoop**
- A half-packed suitcase under the bed. The one-way ticket is in the Bible on the nightstand, not the toiletry bag. The locked bottom drawer holds the Match List (11 names) and Casimir's records.
- A framed 1958 photo of Casimir at his chair, window behind him. The window is exactly the one in her office.
- Phone: the buyer's text; an unplayed voicemail from Priya (*"Don't go before Thursday. Please. I think I found the pattern."*).
- Fridge: *"THU — INDEX?"* with a tooth sticker.
- **Bicuspid** (orange tabby, chipped fang) sits in the lake window. When you come in he **bows his head to the floor**, then leads you to the empty bowl as if showing you your duty.

**Perform: dentistry.** Steady-hand drill (stability meter with the chat as distraction); **X-ray comparison**: overlay the patient's film on Casimir's chart. A match shows as a gold tint. This is the game's first taste of the Case Board.
**Bleed:** says "open wide," studies people's mouths, hums.

**Arc.** *Before:* she found proof she wanted to be wrong about. *Day 1:* eight patients, one of whom (Cal) asks if he knows you. *After:* the Mags Remnant carries on. Bold Mags confronts Delphine with the records; timid Mags keeps selling the practice and never leaves. *Finale:* her Match List is K5's spine.
**Imprint / reacts**
- **High NERVE:** the Remnant gives Delphine the records on Day 4, handing her K3's trigger.
- **High CANDOR:** she tells Cal what she saw, and Cal stops writing in code.
- **Low FIDELITY:** her humming changes, and Silas notices. *"Mags always hums sharp."*
**If returned:** *"Did I miss Friday?"*

**Voice:** *"Open wide, friend. This is the part where I do all the talking."*

## 2. Jonah "Jo" Brask — drummer, 31 · Day 2, Wed 28 Aug

**Truth:** Human. HARROW's drummer; marina hand. **Resonance:** *Gideon "Gid" Brask, 19*, snare drummer in the Mercy Fife & Drum. He climbed the Low Mercy church tower and rang the bell until the water reached him.

**Face:** A gentle giant. Makes pancakes for the band at 3 p.m., says "sorry" to furniture.
**Secret:** The **Reply Tape.** At 4:12 in the Grange Hall basement, when he plays a particular blast-beat, something on the tape *answers*: 212 bell strokes. Jo has never played it for anyone.
**Stance on change:** Hungry for it and ashamed of leaving his sister.
**Decision:** Accepted a slot with a Cleveland band, Salt Cathedral, at 11:58 p.m. and gave notice at the marina.

**Wake & Snoop**
- The kit in the garage, with a wet patch under the floor tom that won't dry. The tape is in a cymbal case.
- A shoebox of Tobi's crayon drawings: *"JO IS A DRAGON."*
- Voicemails: Lux (furious, then not), Daniel Marsh (*"Don't make me find a new fish guy."*).
- **Lemmy** (cockatiel) goes silent, then **drums his beak on the cage bar in the Reply's rhythm** before whistling four notes of Hymn #212.

**Perform: rhythm play.** Practice, a gig at The Low Water, and a hidden track at 4:12 that only appears if the player has found the Tape.
**Bleed:** taps tables; counts people in ("one two one-two-three-four!").

**Arc.** *Before:* a year of quiet misery. *Day 2:* band practice where Lux says *"go, you idiot"* and means *"stay."* *After:* Jo's Remnant remains in HARROW; the band's sound depends on the Notes. *Finale:* the Reply Tape is the sound that wakes the Held to the Raising.
**Imprint / reacts**
- **High WARMTH:** the Remnant becomes the band's glue, and Lux softens.
- **High NERVE:** he finally plays the Reply publicly at the Low Water, drawing Dolores' attention.
- **High CANDOR:** he plays the Tape for Lux, who counts to 212 and goes white.
**If returned:** *"Is it still Wednesday?"*

**Voice:** *"It's not loud. It's just… all of the notes at once."*

## 3. Tobias "Tobi" Okafor-Marsh — 9 · Day 3, Thu 29 Aug

**Truth:** Human. Lamp-bearer for this year's Raising. Son of Adaeze and Daniel. **Resonance:** *Amos Marsh, 9*, the family's lost boy, cousin to Roy Marsh. The boy Casimir was treating.

**Face:** Collects Facts on index cards ("FACTS ABOUT GROWN-UPS"). Double-knots his shoelaces. Corrects adults.
**Secret:** He talks to **Wren** "behind my eyelids." Wren asked him not to carry the lamp. She said it keeps them in.
**Stance on change:** "Change is when you're allowed to leave," says Wren. "Not when you're made to."
**Decision:** At supper, Wed 28 Aug: *"I'm not carrying the lamp. And I'm not going to be the kind of kid who just does what he's told."* A first in 63 years, and a decision about who he is, not just a lamp.

**Wake & Snoop**
- Tobi's room has a periscope made of paper towel tubes, a tin of baby teeth, the practice lamp in the closet. His tooth chart is up on the wall.
- **Sir Hours** (tortoise, gift from Silas) walks to the lake-facing window at tortoise speed, stops, and **raises one front foot, waving.**

**Perform: nine-year-old.** Child-sized camera and paths: vents, under tables, hedges. Adults talk in front of you, because you don't count. A **Honesty Pull** meter: lying beyond a point makes you blurt. Bedtime and homework are real constraints.
**Bleed:** asks "but why?"; double-knots; announces Facts.

**Arc.** *Before:* a sweet, stubborn boy afraid of a lamp. *Day 3:* a day of small decisions that are huge to him; hear what the grown-ups say when they think no one's listening. *After:* the Tobi Remnant is the one who decides the lamp: bold Tobi refuses again; warm Tobi carries it *differently* (he doesn't walk it to the shore; he hands it to someone). *Finale:* the lamp-bearer's choice shapes all endings.
**Imprint / reacts**
- **High WARMTH + NERVE:** at the Raising, the Remnant hands the lamp to Nell. (Opens Ending 4.)
- **Low NERVE:** he carries it and cries. (Pushes Ending 1.)
- **High CURIOSITY:** he asks Roy about Amos; Roy cracks.
**If returned:** Asleep on the shore holding his Facts cards.

**Voice:** *"Fact: grown-ups say 'it's fine' when it isn't. Fact: that's also a fact about me."*

## 4. Mayor Delphine Pruitt — 58 · Day 4, Fri 30 Aug

**Stance on change:** Duty vs penance. She would rather confess and be ruined than keep a lie she inherited.
**Decision:** She will resign after the Raising, ending four generations of Pruitts at the Town Hall, and read the true list as her last act. She signed the letter and put it in the mail Thursday night. Mags was to receive the records the following day.

**Wake & Snoop**

- Family photos: four generations of Pruitts at the Raising, all grim.
- **Gavel** (basset hound) **howls once and lies across your feet**, a very warm obstacle.

**Perform: speeches.** A Town Hall meeting: read the room (mood bar), land rhetorical beats, field Hank's heckles. A one-on-one deal with Pastor Ellis over the Pageant wording.
**Bleed:** *"Neighbors, …"*; two-handed handshakes.

**Arc.** *Before:* four weeks of dread. *Day 4:* the Town Hall; the key. *After:* the Mayor Remnant decides whether the confession happens. Bold Delphine reads the Roll; timid Delphine reads "nothing to report." *Finale:* K3 and the Roll are in her hands.
**Imprint / reacts**

- **High FIDELITY:** she stays "the Mayor" and hardens into the Holdfast's ally.
- **High WARMTH:** she visits Harlan and gets the truth in a single lucid minute.
**If returned:** *"Did I give the speech?"*

**Voice:** *"I'm not saying it's a conspiracy. I'm saying it's a very good filing system."*

## 5. Vernon "Vern" Castellano — radio host, 68 · Day 5, Sat 31 Aug

**Truth:** Human; Index Club. WMRC 88.1's "Vern at Noon" and the Saturday-night **Dedication Hour**. **Resonance:** *Lowell Page, 40*, WMRC's original announcer, who read the evacuation notice on air against orders and signed off *"Fair water, Mercy."*

**Face:** Velvet voice, bow ties, 45s in alphabetical order.
**Secret:** The **dedications** are a code: six songs, in rotation, with the first letters of each month's songs spelling FAIR WATER. And in the archive he found a reel labelled *"SAT 9/9/61 — EVACUATION NOTICE (NOT AIRED)"*, plus Lowell's last broadcast.
**Stance on change:** Truth belongs on the air. He is a loud man in a quiet town.
**Decision:** He will retire. Tonight's Dedication Hour is his last, he told the station owner, and he will close it with Hymn #212.

**Wake & Snoop**
- A home studio of cables and cat hair. The hymn on reel, cued.
- Letters from listeners in impossible handwriting. A Dead Letter Box slip from Connie.
- **Static** (deaf white cat) **presses her whole body against the speaker cabinet** and goes still, whiskers pointed toward the lake.

**Perform: radio.** Cue records, read dedications, take call-ins on air without flinching. The calls leak tells, if you listen.
**Bleed:** *"That was… and next…"*; talks to invisible microphones.

**Arc.** *Before:* two years of decoding. *Day 5:* **the Act I climax.** Air the hymn, or don't. If aired, walk-ons across town Awaken in front of their families; that night the first Awakened looks straight at you. *After:* the Vern Remnant hosts "the Dedication Hour," and the dedications change according to the player's Notes. *Finale:* the radio is how the Raising reaches the Held.
**Imprint / reacts**
- **Hymn aired + High NERVE:** the Waking gains four walk-ons overnight; Dolores's Holdfast begins sabotage.
- **Hymn not aired:** Vern keeps the reel; Priya finds it on Day 7.
- **High CURIOSITY:** the Remnant starts dedicating songs to named walk-ons, which is dangerous.
**If returned:** *"Was I on air?"*

**Voice:** *"This one goes out to everyone who is waiting. You know who you are. So does the lake."*

## 6. Augustin "Gus" Tamm — launch captain, 67 · Day 6, Sun 1 Sept

**Truth:** Human. Widower. Master of the launch *Mercy Ann*, the only way across to the Far Landing and the bus. **Resonance:** *Capt. Anders Tamm, 54*, his grandfather. In 1961 he ferried 41 people across the river in the surge, then went back for the next load.

**Face:** Gruff, funny, names the waves. Calls passengers "ballast."
**Secret:** Ingrid's ashes (died 2011, no funeral) have been in a thermos in the bilge locker for thirteen years. And **he has seen the lights**: in fog the launch passes the drowned spire, and a lamp in the water follows the wake.
**Stance on change:** Weary. He wants to be done with staying.
**Decision:** To cross with Ingrid, scatter her on the Far shore, and not come back.

**Wake & Snoop**
- Charts, tide-less tide tables, 60 years of logbooks. The thermos. Ingrid's reading glasses on the sill.
- **Port** (three-legged border collie) **sits and salutes with his paw**, then turns to face the lake.

**Perform: piloting.** Cross in fog by bell-buoy, light and count. Passenger conversations on the way; they're the cheapest place in town to watch for tells.
**Bleed:** nautical idiom; *"Mind the gap, ballast."*

**Arc.** *Before:* thirteen years unmourned. *Day 6:* a crossing where the lamp in the water appears. *After:* the Gus Remnant either finishes the crossing or turns back. *Finale:* the *Mercy Ann* carries the 212 across at the Raising in Ending 4.
**Imprint / reacts**

- **High CURIOSITY:** he follows the lamp.

## Remnants in other lives: how each reacts to what you did elsewhere

Once the Chorus steps out of a Life at dusk, that person's Remnant is a walk-on in town who lives on. They react in later days to the Chorus *as someone else*, by how the player has behaved in other roles. These are the bespoke lines that sit on top of the generic ripple rules (`04-relationship-web.md` §5).

| Remnant | If you are warm / bold / curious in another role, they … | If you are cold / timid / incurious, they … |
