**Rules for a good cross-reference**
1. The seed must be funny or lovely *on its own*.
2. The pay-off must be an *object or a sound*, never a line of dialogue.
3. Both ends must be visible on the Case Board as distinct nodes.
4. At least two cross-references must pay off in **every** ending (#9, #21).

## 2. Reframe ladders (examples of "charming → horrifying")

**The blue stripe**
L1 *A pretty town* → L2 *Hank repaints it every spring* → L3 *It's the high-water mark* → L4 *The Rise is built on the same contour as Low Mercy*, so the stripe is the *ceiling of a house that is still down there*.

**"Same time tomorrow"**
L1 *Friendly* → L2 *Nobody ever says goodbye, even when someone's leaving* → L3 *It's what the Stayers said across the valley* → Ω *Fair water was what they actually meant.*

**The hum**
L1 *Bea hums while she bakes* → L2 *Several people hum the same four notes* → L3 *It's from the drowned Chapel's hymnal* → L4 *Those who hum it are walk-ons* → L5 *The Chorus hums it too, and the player hears it from the first morning.*
